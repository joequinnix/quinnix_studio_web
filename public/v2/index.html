What I
do/

Five disciplines, one connected process — from trend to shelf.

  • Full collections for sport, lifestyle and fashion — concept, materials, colour, tech specs and sign-off for mass production.

  • Hands-on management with factories and suppliers: cost, lead time, samples and quality through to delivery.

  • Quarterly global-trend presentations and range planning tailored to regional markets and buyers.

  • Logos, athlete-collection branding, posters, catalogs and illustration that carry a collection across every touchpoint.

  • Retail packaging, in-store displays, print campaigns and advertising banners.
